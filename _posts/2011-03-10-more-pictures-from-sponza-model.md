Two more views of Sponza in the Glow engine editor, following yesterday's close-up of the lion.

The first looks down from the upper gallery. Through the arch at the bottom you can see the lion relief lit at the far end, and the blue and red curtains hang between the columns.

[![Sponza seen from the upper gallery in the Glow engine editor](/assets/img/2011/editor_sponza3-312bcc.jpg)](/assets/img/2011/editor_sponza3-a7ddf7.jpg)

The second is on the ground floor among the columns, where yellow and red lights fall on the vaults and the curtains sit in the middle of the frame. The lighting in both shots comes from the square_light entities listed at the bottom of the editor.

[![Sponza ground floor with warm coloured lights on the vaults](/assets/img/2011/editor_sponza2-59398a.jpg)](/assets/img/2011/editor_sponza2-a2824d.jpg)
