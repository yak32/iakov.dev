I've been racking my brain for a fresh game idea. It's harder than I thought.

I have one idea, maybe a stupid one: an atomic bombing, shown in detail. In the first part you are the pilot of the bomber. After that you are a civilian escaping from the town, with burning people and death everywhere. The game would be about how terrible that is. It would be short, made specifically for the IGF, and closer to an interactive movie than a normal game.

I'd do it all in real 3D, with plenty of detail. Or maybe in 2D for the IGF, since they seem to like 2D games there.
