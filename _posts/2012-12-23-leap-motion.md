It looks like I'm going to receive a [Leap Motion controller](https://leapmotion.com/). I applied for a developer unit some time ago and had forgotten about it.

The Leap Motion is a small device that sits in front of the keyboard and tracks your hands and fingers in the air above it, so you can control the computer with gestures without touching anything. The technology looks promising. It reminds me of the interfaces in Minority Report.
