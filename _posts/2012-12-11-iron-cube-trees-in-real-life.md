It turns out the trees from Iron Cube exist.

The photo above is an avenue in a park where the trees are clipped into tall green boxes on bare trunks. They stand in straight rows on both sides of a mown lawn, with a gravel path crossing in front.

In Iron Cube every tree is a brown voxel trunk with a green block on top, which is about the simplest tree a voxel world can have. Someone has grown and trimmed the same shape for real.
