As I [wrote earlier](/2012/12/leap-motion/), I applied to the Leap Motion developer programme and was selected as a developer. The device arrived today and I ran some tests. It looks responsive and precise, as you can see in the video. Later I'll try to integrate the Leap SDK into the Glow engine. It seems perfect for manipulating 3D meshes, or for shooters.

![The FedEx parcel with the Leap Motion, addressed to Glow Engine](/assets/img/2013/01/IMG_5649.jpg)

Unboxing:

![The Leap Motion box, labelled Dev Board V.05](/assets/img/2013/01/IMG_5656.jpg)

It's a kind of multi-touch without the actual touching. Multi-touch support in the Android port of Glow is already done. By the way, the Android port of the Glow engine and the Iron Cube game is going very well, and I'll show some of it soon.
