I've made a first version of a random landscape generator in the editor. It's built on the open-source (LGPL) [libnoise](http://libnoise.sourceforge.net/) library and can create single random landscapes as well as grids of them.

Later I'll add simple editing tools, plus generation of bump, detail and weight maps. The video shows the generator working inside the editor.
