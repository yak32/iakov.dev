This screenshot is a small test scene from my tank game for the Intel Ultrabook contest. A single block of voxel terrain floats over the sea, with one cubic tree and the tank parked next to it. The block is cut off at the edges, so you can see the layers of the voxel field: grass and sand on top, dark earth underneath.
