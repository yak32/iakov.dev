I ported the Glow engine to Android over the last few weekends. To be honest, the port is based on the [Google Native Client port](/2012/10/google-nacl-build-of-glow-engine/) of the engine, and recently I've spent a lot of time making Glow properly cross-platform. I'll write more about it later.

My new Galaxy Note II is great, and I'll write about it some time as well. It's fast: it loads the voxel scene from my game in a time comparable to the PC.
