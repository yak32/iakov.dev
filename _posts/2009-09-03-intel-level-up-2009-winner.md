I won Intel Level Up 2009. I Know Your Deeds, the one-level demo I finished after three nights without sleep, took first place in Best Game Optimized for Intel® Graphics and second place in Best Threaded Game. I can hardly believe it.

The judges were some of the best-known game developers and designers in the world. You can see the list here: [http://software.intel.com/en-us/articles/levelup-judges/](http://software.intel.com/en-us/articles/levelup-judges/)

Thanks, Intel.
