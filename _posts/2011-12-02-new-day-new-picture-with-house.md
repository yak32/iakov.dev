I'm playing with the combination of atmospheric scattering, light intensity, a dynamic sun and the time of day.

![White houses with blue doors on green hills under a low evening sun](/assets/img/2011/glow_houses-9b1684.jpg)

The test scene is a row of white houses with blue doors and windows, red and white awnings and stone steps, standing on grassy hills. The sun is low, so the sky goes from grey-blue overhead to yellow at the horizon. The sun and sky follow the Time of Day and Length of Day scene properties I added yesterday.
