I'm running automated tests on Iron Cube. Every second the test picks a random action: move forward or backward, turn left or right, or shoot.

I took this screenshot after several hours of testing. The ground is covered in craters from all the random shots, which is a decent stress test for the destructible voxel terrain. To my surprise there were no crashes and no other problems, so I'm ready to upload the new version to the Google Play store.
