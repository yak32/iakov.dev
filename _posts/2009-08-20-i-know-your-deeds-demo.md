[Here is my current demo](http://haemoglobin-game.com/IknowYourDeedsSetup.msi) (about 50 MB). It has one level, and for now there is more graphics than gameplay in it. This is the build I submitted to the Intel Level Up 2009 competition.

Your goal is to find cover before night falls. Close the doors, and use boxes to build barricades.

Run the "best threaded" shortcut for the best graphics. If your machine has integrated graphics, use the "Intel graphics" shortcut.

I'm carrying on with the game. I've found a talented artist, and I'm implementing networking.
