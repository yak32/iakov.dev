Today I added atmospheric scattering back into the engine (it existed before and I've restored it). It's dynamic and renders beautiful sunsets and dawns. It comes with two new scene properties: Time of Day, from 0 to 24 in hours, and Length of Day, also in hours. The screenshot above goes through the HDR pipeline I finished yesterday.
