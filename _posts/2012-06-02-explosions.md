I spent a bit of time playing with grenades and increased the radius of the explosions. The video above shows how they look now, blowing holes in the voxel terrain.

Everything in Tarta's world can be destroyed this way, because the whole level is a voxel field. Since last week the server is the one that edits that field, and it sends the changed chunks to the clients over the network.
