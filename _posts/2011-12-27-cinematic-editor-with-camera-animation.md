The camera can now be animated like any other entity in the editor. Drag the frame slider, then change the object's position, orientation or scale, and the editor creates an animation key. Between key frames the object's position is interpolated.

The frame slider is the "frames" bar along the bottom of the editor, next to the auto, prev and next buttons. The cinematic editor was one of the features I listed [a few days ago](/2011/12/a-lot-of-new-features-implemented/), and this video shows it at work.
