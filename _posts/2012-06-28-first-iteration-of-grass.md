I've been playing with grass these days, and the screenshot above is the first iteration. The grass sits on top of the voxel terrain at the edge of a dug-out pit, with the blocky earth walls behind it.

The texture is the part I'm not happy with. It's a soft, realistic grass, and next to the hard edges of the voxels it looks like it came from another game. I'll change it to something more cubic so it fits the rest of the world.
