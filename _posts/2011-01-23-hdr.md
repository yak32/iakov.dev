The Glow engine now has [high dynamic range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_dynamic_range_imaging) lighting with eye adaptation, so the exposure adjusts over time as the brightness of the scene changes. Rendering is also [gamma-correct](http://filmicgames.com/archives/299) now.

I have also started on [colour transformations](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems2/part-iii-high-quality-rendering/chapter-24-using-lookup-tables-accelerate-color) (hue, saturation, brightness and so on). These are post-processing effects, like the motion blur from last month.
