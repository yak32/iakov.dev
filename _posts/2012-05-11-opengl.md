Tarta now runs in OpenGL, and this video shows it moving. Yesterday I posted the [first screenshot](/2012/05/first-opengl-screen/) from the new renderer.

There's still a lot of work left, but I hope to finish it soon. The OpenGL renderer is what will take the Glow engine to Linux and macOS, and later to iOS and Android, with the HLSL shaders converted by hlsl2glsl.
