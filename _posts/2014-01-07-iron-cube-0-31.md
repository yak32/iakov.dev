Iron Cube 0.31 is out for Windows and Android. It's a multiplayer tank combat game in a voxel world.

[![Download Iron Cube 0.32 from Mod DB](http://button.moddb.com/download/medium/64106.png)](http://www.moddb.com/games/iron-cube/downloads/iron-cube-032)

New features and bug fixes:

* 3 different levels
* 2 tanks with different features
* the map is now shown on screen
* reliable online play for 2 players (for now)
* your own tank is simulated locally on the client, so there is no lag
* a game lasts until you or your enemy has died 5 times, and enemies respawn on the opposite side of the map
* new title screen
* much faster startup
* damage indicator
* 3D sound
* friendly bots are disabled for now, but they will come back soon
